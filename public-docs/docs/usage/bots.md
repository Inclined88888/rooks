## The view you land on

**TIMs** is where Timon opens once onboarding is over: your main TIM's chat, ready to talk to, with the list of your TIMs beside it. A TIM is one of your agents that belongs to no project; the list groups them by team, the main TIM pinned at the top, centred. The dot after a name shows what the TIM is doing (working, waiting for an answer).

The screen is made of two islands: the list on the left, the chat on the rest. Pick a TIM in the list and its chat replaces the one on the right, with no page change; the back button returns to the TIM you had open. A link to `/bots?agent=<name>` opens that TIM's chat directly (the address keeps its former name, so links and saved layouts still work).

## Arrange it like any view

The list is a widget shown in an island of its own. On **TIMs** neither the list nor the chat has a title bar: the view reads as one page, built around the list, which is not moved or closed there. Drag the gutter to widen it, or open something else beside the chat. Closed, the list comes back from **Show the TIMs list** at the top of the chat. Brought down to the chat alone, the view lays the list back on your next visit.

On a phone, the list opens as a drawer from the same button.

## Use the list on another view

The **TIMs** card is in the widget gallery: drop it on the cockpit, in a sidebar or in an island of any view. A click on a TIM there opens its chat on that view, without leaving it: beside the list when the list is in an island, as a tab of the active island otherwise. A TIM whose chat is already open is brought forward instead of being opened twice.

## Search, create a TIM or a team

The bar at the foot of the list searches the TIMs by name or role, and holds **New TIM** (the agent editor), **New team** and **Import agents**.

The **…** of a TIM, or a right click on it, opens its menu: **Move to a team** lists the teams (with **Back** to return), **Leave its team** takes it out, and **Export** downloads it alone as a `.zip` another Timon imports. You can also drag a TIM onto a team, or onto **No team**. A right click on the empty space of the list offers **New TIM** and **New team**. The **…** of a team's heading, or a right click on it, renames or deletes the team; deleting a team keeps its TIMs.
